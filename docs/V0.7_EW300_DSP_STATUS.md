## Evidence already available

The preserved evidence from PR #21 and its discovery history establishes:

- exact observed identity: VID:PID `31B2:0111`, revision `0101`, manufacturer `LE XIAN`, product `SIMGOT EW300 DSP`;
- HID interface 3 and the captured 74-byte report descriptor;
- vendor report IDs `0x4B` and `0x54` with 10-byte input/output payload declarations;
- exact read framing using report `0x4B`, command `0x52`, and register echoes;
- a preserved untouched stock snapshot containing the current slot, five observed band pairs, and a global-gain payload;
- volatile gain/frequency/Q raw-field write, readback, exact restoration, and final full-snapshot matching on the owner’s cable;
- reversible raw filter-code experiments for codes 1–4 with exact restoration; and
- Android diagnostic launchability hardening, including DEX class-definition and cold-launch checks.

These facts qualify the observed raw transport boundaries. They do not, by themselves, qualify acoustic units, full ranges, persistence, Reset, or every retail revision.

## Remaining hardware gate

No additional discovery loop is planned. Public research and automated analysis resolved the
frequency and playback-gain classification questions but cannot prove nonvolatile Save behavior.
The remaining capability resolution is therefore embedded in the single consolidated signed-beta
session rather than scheduled as a separate test loop. Its two complete power-removal checks prove
temporary persistence and exact baseline restoration before any normal Flash/Reset validation.

The owner is not asked to test until:

1. all software and security gates pass on one exact source commit;
2. the APK package, signer, checksum, and launchability are verified;
3. all unsupported capabilities are explicitly absent from the UI; and
4. one concise product-language checklist is ready.

The previous signed candidate satisfied those software-side prerequisites, but its Apply attempt
was stopped before mutation. The exported report proves zero register writes and zero Save commands.
The next and only planned physical work remains the single consolidated session in
`docs/EW300_DSP_HANDS_ON_CHECKLIST.md`, after the replacement candidate passes the complete gates.

## Current implementation boundary

The full-build continuation contains guarded readback and Peak-only capture for the observed
five-band transport, a versioned exact-fingerprint capability profile, and a shared strict
baseline/restore coordinator for Apply, Flash, and Reset. The optimizer remains source-neutral
across OPRA, AutoEQ, community/general sources, imports, Personal EQs, and DAC captures. Any
volume, headset, UAC, microphone, firmware, bootloader, or other DAC control that the exact
hardware profile genuinely supports must be exposed through the same capability-driven surface.
No such additional support is established for this EW300 fingerprint, so those controls remain
absent and unclaimed in this release; no unrelated Black Pearl controls are inherited.

The normal product UI no longer exposes the historical mutating qualification action. Apply,
persistent Flash, and Reset are guarded by the exact qualified fingerprint, active Direct Flash
setting, current connection, complete baseline, one Save command, and final readback. An uncertain
mutation is terminal and is not retried. No public release claim is made. No factory-reset claim
is made. No Black Pearl control is copied to EW300.
