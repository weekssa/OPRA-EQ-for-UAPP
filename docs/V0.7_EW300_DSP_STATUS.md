## Current implementation boundary

The full-build continuation contains guarded readback and Peak-only capture for the observed
five-band transport, a versioned exact-fingerprint capability profile, and a shared strict
baseline/restore coordinator for Apply, Flash, and Reset. The optimizer remains source-neutral
across OPRA, AutoEQ, community/general sources, imports, Personal EQs, and DAC captures. Any
volume, headset, UAC, microphone, firmware, bootloader, or other DAC control that the exact
hardware profile genuinely supports must be exposed through the same capability-driven surface.
No such additional support is established for this EW300 fingerprint, so those controls remain
absent and unclaimed in this release; no unrelated Black Pearl controls are inherited.

The public product UI no longer exposes the historical mutating qualification action or the
validation report/share controls. My DAC → EQ is the usable five-band Peak path: readback, local
editing, Review → Apply, Peak-only capture, Reset EQ to flat, and verified reconnect/final-readback
feedback. My EQs and EQ Library remain the Flash surfaces.

My DAC → DEVICE reports only the exact profile's verified playback/global-gain state, active
Peak-band count, and current/last-read connection freshness. It does not inherit Black Pearl
controls or claim unsupported EW300 functions. Apply, persistent Flash, and Reset remain guarded
by the exact qualified fingerprint, active Direct Flash setting, current connection, complete
baseline, one Save command, and final readback. The signed validation candidate alone exposes
capability/operation report sharing and exact post-Save baseline restoration; these controls are
not part of the public release. An uncertain mutation is terminal and is not retried. No factory
reset claim is made. No Black Pearl control is copied to EW300.
