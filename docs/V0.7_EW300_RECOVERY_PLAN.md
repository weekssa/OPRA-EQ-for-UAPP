## Evidence and safety contract

Only exact-device evidence may promote a capability to the product surface.
Unknown controls are omitted. Writes require an exact identity, fresh baseline,
reviewable transaction, exact readback, restoration on failure, and no mutating
retry. Persistence and Reset are not called successful until their exact-device
semantics are proven.

The hardware budget is: no new discovery loop; at most one bounded
capability-resolution session if a necessary feature cannot be resolved from
existing evidence or public research; then one consolidated physical validation
session after the complete signed beta passes all software gates.
