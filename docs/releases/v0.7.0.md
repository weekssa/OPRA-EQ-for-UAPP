# v0.7.0 — draft, not released

Status: software implementation and release-candidate hardening are in progress on
`feature/v0.7-ew300-luna-high` / draft PR #23. No merge, publication, or public EW300 support
claim is authorized.

Scope is the exact SIMGOT EW300 DSP identity (`31B2:0111`, `LE XIAN`, `SIMGOT EW300 DSP`, HID
interface 3) through the existing capability-driven My DAC flow: five-band direct-Hz Peak EQ,
readback, capture, guarded Apply, one-Save Flash, Reset, reconnect-aware verification, and
privacy-safe readable/JSON operation evidence. Playback gain remains device state and is not part
of canonical captured EQs. Other device controls, unknown revisions, firmware, and VID/PID-only
matches remain unsupported.

Accepted historical evidence is recorded in `docs/EW300_VALIDATION_LEDGER.md`. The current branch
fixes a verified permission-flow race by blocking automatic reconnect until the Save send has
completed and by recording permission/Save/readback invariants. The exact signed candidate must
pass the complete software, security, signing, installation, upgrade, launch, accessibility, and
release-polish gates before the one consolidated physical session in
`docs/EW300_DSP_HANDS_ON_CHECKLIST.md`.

Prepared publication package, pending owner approval:

- source commit, signed APK, SHA-256, signer, candidate manifest, and CI links from one frozen run;
- hardware reports and restored-state result from the single consolidated session;
- known limitations and rollback guidance from the capability matrix; and
- explicit go/no-go decision. Merge, publish, and public support remain owner-controlled.

The shipping recovery branch does not include the separate discovery APK/tooling. Its preserved evidence is documented in `docs/EW300_DSP_PROTOCOL_NOTES.md` and `docs/V0.7_EW300_DSP_STATUS.md`.

Publication and merge require the completed protocol, regression and signed-candidate gates followed by the owner's exact-candidate physical PASS and explicit authorization.
