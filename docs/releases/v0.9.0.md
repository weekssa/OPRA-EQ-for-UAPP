# EQ Library v0.9.0

**Status: draft release notes, pending owner GO.** Do not publish before the documented gates and the
owner approval.

## Highlights

- **FiiO JA11 saved settings now work.** The selected built-in EQ program (Vocal, Classic, Bass, User 1,
  Off) and the device volume can be saved as the JA11 startup defaults and survive a real unplug and
  replug. The save uses the JA11 native preset-specific transaction, keeps the active EQ and the
  microphone setting intact, and never cycles the microphone.
- **Runtime and saved state are distinct.** A runtime change applies immediately and is labelled as
  session-only until you choose Set as Default; a verified save is labelled as saved for startup.
- **Digital filters are not part of v0.9.0.** The JA11 firmware tested (2.20) does not expose a
  reconstruction-filter control through the official vendor interface, so the control is not shown.
  The research is preserved for a future version.

## FiiO JA11 details

- Set as Default writes the active five-band bank and global gain back to the JA11, applies once, saves
  once and verifies the result, tolerating one brief re-enumeration.
- The JA11 may disconnect for a moment while saving; the app re-reads and verifies afterward.
- Unplugging the JA11 restores its saved startup program and volume; unsaved runtime changes revert.
- The custom User 1 bank and global gain are preserved; no flat curve or helper preset is substituted.
- No microphone or UAC changes are made by the defaults transaction.

## Known limitations

- JA11 digital reconstruction filters are unavailable on firmware 2.20 through the vendor protocol;
  the control is deferred (see `docs/research/FIIO_JA11_DIGITAL_FILTER_RESEARCH_HANDOFF.md`).
- Persistence was physically qualified on one Pixel 9 and one JA11 at firmware 2.20.
- The qualified defaults transaction re-stages the active bank for built-in programs; a smaller
  vendor-aligned sequence remains a future optimisation pending its own qualification.

## Upgrade and installation

- Public stable remains v0.8.3 until this release is published; installing v0.9.0 upgrades the existing
  app and keeps local data.
- Official signing and the exact artifact are produced by the main-only release workflow after merge.
